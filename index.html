South Tenerife

More than another Tenerife excursion.

Escape the resort for a while and discover memorable landscapes, local experiences and Atlantic adventures. Tenerife Hidden Side is designed for couples, friends, families and small groups who want to experience something different during their stay.

Experience 01

Volcanic Coast & Los Gigantes Adventure

Swim. Explore. Sail.

  1. 01

    Volcanic Coast

    Enjoy time around Tenerife's beautiful volcanic coastline, with dramatic dark rock, clear Atlantic water and memorable places to relax, photograph and enjoy the surroundings.

  2. 02

    Los Gigantes

    Continue toward one of Tenerife's most spectacular coastal landscapes and experience the enormous cliffs of Los Gigantes.

  3. 03

    Atlantic Boat Experience

    Join a local boat experience and head out beneath the cliffs.

    • Whale and dolphin watching
    • Impressive cliff views
    • Ocean scenery
    • Coastal exploration
    • An opportunity for an ocean swimming stop

    Depending on the selected boat excursion, weather, wildlife and sea conditions. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

  4. 04

    Finish the Experience

    Finish the day with time to enjoy the atmosphere before returning toward South Tenerife.

Final meeting and travel details are confirmed directly after reservation. Wildlife sightings depend on natural conditions and can never be guaranteed.
